## Target audience
Business owners (Pakistan-focused but broadly applicable) considering an AI chatbot for their website/WhatsApp — comparing options, unsure what it should cost or what tech is involved. Non-technical readers.

## Required structure
1. **Direct-answer opening paragraph** (AEO-critical) — answer "how much does a chatbot cost" in the first 2-3 sentences with a real range, before any preamble.
2. Clear H1, scannable H2/H3 headings throughout.
3. **Cost breakdown section** — by chatbot complexity tier (e.g. simple FAQ bot vs lead-capture bot with CRM/WhatsApp integration vs advanced multi-language/voice bot), with realistic PKR and USD ranges.
4. **Tech stack section** — explain, in plain language, the real components involved (LLM/AI provider choice, Next.js/backend, database for lead storage, WhatsApp Business API, email automation, CRM integration) — use Avenix Studio's own Nova project as a real-world example/mini case study of what a production chatbot stack actually looks like (Groq for speed, Supabase for storage, Resend for email, HubSpot for CRM, Meta Cloud API for WhatsApp — described in plain terms, not as an ad).
5. **What affects the price** — a factors list (number of integrations, lead capture complexity, calendar/WhatsApp automation, multilingual support, ongoing maintenance).
6. **Comparison table**: DIY/no-code tools vs custom-built chatbot (like Nova) vs enterprise platforms — cost, flexibility, ownership tradeoffs.
7. **FAQ section** at the end (schema-friendly) — 5-6 real questions people search ("is a chatbot worth it for small business", "how long does it take to build a chatbot", "chatbot vs live chat", etc.)
8. Soft CTA at the end pointing to Avenix Studio's chatbot/AI automation services — no hard sell.
