## 8. Reviews — the highest-leverage ongoing task

- [ ] Get your GBP review link (Profile → Ask for reviews) and save it somewhere you
      will actually find it
- [ ] Ask every satisfied client, at the moment the work is delivered and they are happy
- [ ] Send the direct link, not instructions. Every extra step loses people
- [ ] Reply to all reviews, including negative ones. A measured reply to a complaint
      reassures the next reader more than an unbroken wall of five stars
- [ ] **Never buy reviews.** Detectable, removable, and the penalty is worse than the
      problem it solves

A steady trickle of genuine reviews will, over a few months, put you ahead of nearly
every competitor who asks sporadically.
